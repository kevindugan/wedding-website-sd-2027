Our Story

December 2020, the world was more used to the pandemic, there was hope in the air that permeated even the n95s. Julia and Kevin had both been trying virtual dating to decrease risks, and to weed out potential duds. Julia had been working double masked in the hospital as an Ob/Gyn resident, making trips to the ICU with patients but still helping babies into the world. Kevin had been working from home for months, seeing people in person briefly when he would go grocery shopping, but getting good at virtual water cooler chit chat. The app put them in each other's paths, and the virtual dates to Carnegie Hall went well. So, after Julia was fully vaccinated, they met in person and have kept meeting in person ever since! Now, after a move to California for Julia's Gynecologic Oncology fellowship, getting a second cat together (because Julia's cat Gigi wasn't enough of a personality), and countless trips locally, nationally, and internationally together and with family and friends, they're excited to celebrate their relationship with you!

What's your favorite activity together?

We love to cook meals together! We'll switch off who is chef and sous chef. Kevin makes killer sauces, and Julia ensures there's plenty of delicious vegetables involved. We also love a good afternoon curled up with a book or working side by side on research or coding projects.

What's the most memorable trip you have taken together?

The engagement trip to Mammoth last fall (see "Moments" page in the drop down menu) was pretty spectacular. We got away for the weekend and spent time hiking and enjoying the beautiful scenery together.
