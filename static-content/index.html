Our Story

Think back to December 2020, a dark cold Pennsylvania Delta wave winter, but with the promise of vaccines a-comin'. Julia had been working double masked in the hospital as an Ob/Gyn resident, making trips to the ICU with patients but still helping babies into the world. Kevin had been working from home for months, seeing people in person briefly when he would go grocery shopping, but getting good at virtual water cooler chit chat. A dating app put them in each other's paths, and the virtual dates to Carnegie Hall went well. So, after Julia was fully vaccinated, they met in person and have kept meeting in person ever since!

They moved in together in California for Julia's Gynecologic Oncology fellowship, adopted a second cat together (because Julia's cat Gigi wasn't enough of a personality), and traveled a lot - Maine, New York, Baltimore, Texas, Oregon, Mexico, England, and so many other places. A lot of that travel was for weddings of friends and family, so eventually they planned their own wedding in Texas.

Life had other plans, though, so the official wedding was a quieter affair planned in between Julia's chemotherapy cycles. Now cancer-free, they're excited to celebrate with you. Come join them in San Diego to celebrate the wedding, a year of cancer-free, Julia graduating and starting a new job, Kevin starting a new job, and the cats surviving over 3 years together without bloodshed.

What was the engagement story?

We did a weekend trip to Mammoth where we spent time hiking, enjoying the scenery, and talking about our relationship and goals. We also took a LOT of pictures (see some of them below).

What about the wedding?

Julia was still immunocompromised, so we had two local friends over for homemade pizza and cake to sign the paperwork (thanks, Roshni and Vishwa!).

Any honeymoon plans?

We took our honeymoon/graduation/celebration trip in August 2026. We had been planning it for a year, and are grateful the timing worked out with all of Julia's treatments and training so we were able to spend 3 weeks enjoying Southern Africa. The safaris in Chobe and Kruger were an absolute highlight, and we're excited to bring some of that to you with our venue!

Travel

If you're flying in, we recommend the San Diego International airport. It's about 30 minutes from the airport to Escondido. If you're wanting to explore the area, we do recommend renting a car, as there aren't great public transportation options to Escondido. Uber and Lyft do service the area well.

There's a ton to explore here for you to get to enjoy in the Southern California sunshine. Some highlights (with times from Escondido)

  • Legoland California resort in Carlsbad (about 30 minutes)
  • The Zoo and Safari Park, obviously!
  • Explore the beaches - Encinitas, Del Mar, Torrey Pines, La Jolla, etc.
  • San Diego Botanical Garden in Encinitas (about 30 minutes)
  • Balboa Park in downtown San Diego (about 30 minutes) has a bunch of museums and parks
  • Cabrillo National monument (about 45 minutes)
  • Explore wine country (wineries around Escondido extending up to Temecula, about 30 minutes away)
  • USS Midway museum (about 40 minutes)
  • Eat some tacos! In Escondido, try Mesa Agricola, TJ Tacos, or Craft and Taco Lounge!

Q & A

Are kids welcome?

Yes! All ages are welcome at all events. There's also options for seclusion for those who are breastfeeding.

Can I bring a date who was not named on the invitation?

Invitations are to named guests only. Please reach out to clarify if you're uncertain.

What options are there for parking and transportation?

Your invitation to the Safari Park includes free parking and entry to the park on the day. We'll be arranging shuttles from the SpringHills Suites in Escondido so people can enjoy the libations freely. The Safari Park does allow cars to be left overnight, but recommends these are picked up by 8am the following day, or there might be fees involved.

How will I get in the park?

Closer to the date, we'll email you a voucher for parking. If you wish to explore the park earlier on Saturday, your tickets will be at Will Call. To get back in, you'll need a hand stamp and the ticket. If you arrive just for the event, there will be a table set up with tickets under your name starting at 3pm.

What time should I arrive on the day?

Doors open at 3:00PM, we recommend you arrive around 3:45PM to get settled and say hi. There are trams from the welcome table at the front of the park to the venue (from Hunte Pavilion to Kijami Overlook, if you're looking at a map). We'll be starting at 4:30PM.

What shoes should I wear? What's the terrain like?

Dancing is expected at the event, and there may even be a dance lesson to help you get your groove on. Please make sure you've got shoes you can move in! There will be a mixture of hard surfaces and grass.

I'm having mobility issues - can I still get there and get around?

Absolutely! Please let us know, and the Zoo staff can work with you to get you to the event in style.

Is there a gift registry?

We have most of what we need, and are truly looking forward to celebrating with you. Gifts are not expected, but we will have a place to leave cards.
