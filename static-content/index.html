Our Story

December 2020, the world was more used to the pandemic, there was hope in the air that permeated even the n95s. Julia and Kevin had both been trying virtual dating to decrease risks, and to weed out potential duds. Julia had been working double masked in the hospital as an Ob/Gyn resident, making trips to the ICU with patients but still helping babies into the world. Kevin had been working from home for months, seeing people in person briefly when he would go grocery shopping, but getting good at virtual water cooler chit chat. The app put them in each other's paths, and the virtual dates to Carnegie Hall went well. So, after Julia was fully vaccinated, they met in person and have kept meeting in person ever since! Now, after a move to California for Julia's Gynecologic Oncology fellowship, getting a second cat together (because Julia's cat Gigi wasn't enough of a personality), and countless trips locally, nationally, and internationally together and with family and friends, they're excited to celebrate their relationship with you!

What's your favorite activity together?

We love to cook meals together! We'll switch off who is chef and sous chef. Kevin makes killer sauces, and Julia ensures there's plenty of delicious vegetables involved. We also love a good afternoon curled up with a book or working side by side on research or coding projects.

What's the most memorable trip you have taken together?

The engagement trip to Mammoth last fall (see "Moments" page in the drop down menu) was pretty spectacular. We got away for the weekend and spent time hiking and enjoying the beautiful scenery together.

Travel

If you're flying in, we recommend the San Diego International airport. It's about 30 minutes from the airport to Escondido. If you're wanting to explore the area, we do recommend renting a car, as there aren't great public transportation options to Escondido. Uber and Lyft do service the area well.

There's a ton to explore here for you to get to enjoy in the Southern California sunshine. Some highlights (with times from Escondido)

  • Legoland California resort in Carlsbad (about 30 minutes)
  • The Zoo and Safari Park, obviously!
  • Explore the beaches - Encinitas, Del Mar, Torrey Pines, La Jolla, etc.
  • San Diego Botanical Garden in Encinitas (about 30 minutes)
  • Balboa Park in downtown San Diego (about 30 minutes) has a bunch of museums and parks
  • Cabrillo National monument (about 45 minutes)
  • Explore wine country (wineries around Escondido extending up to Temecula, about 30 minutes away)
  • USS Midway museum (about 40 minutes)
  • Eat some tacos! In Escondido, try Mesa Agricola, TJ Tacos, or Craft and Taco Lounge!

Q & A

Are kids welcome?

Yes! All ages are welcome at all events. There's also options for seclusion for those who are breastfeeding.

Can I bring a date who was not named on the invitation?

Invitations are to named guests only. Please reach out to clarify if you're uncertain.

What options are there for parking and transportation?

Your invitation to the Safari Park includes free parking and entry to the park. We'll be arranging shuttles from the SpringHills Suites in Escondido so people can enjoy the libations freely. The Safari Park does allow cars to be left overnight, but recommends these are picked up by 8am the following day, or there might be fees involved.

How will I get in the park?

Closer to the date, we'll email you a voucher for parking. If you wish to explore the park earlier on Saturday, your tickets will be at Will Call. If you arrive just for the event, there will be a table set up with tickets under your name.

What time should I arrive on the day?

Doors open at 3:30PM, we recommend you arrive around 4:00PM to get settled and say hi. We'll be starting at 4:30PM

What shoes should I wear? What's the terrain like?

Dancing is expected at the event, and there may even be a dance lesson to help you get your groove on. Please make sure you've got shoes you can move in! There will be a mixture of hard surfaces and grass.

I'm having mobility issues - can I still get there and get around?

Absolutely! Please let us know, and the Zoo staff can work with you to get you to the event in style.

Is there a gift registry?

We have most of what we need, and are truly looking forward to celebrating with you. Gifts are not expected.
